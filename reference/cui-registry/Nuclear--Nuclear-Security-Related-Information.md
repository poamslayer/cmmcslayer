Nuclear Security-Related Information

  
Category Abbreviation:  SRI

Category Description:  Information that could be useful, or could reasonably be expected to be useful, to a terrorist in a potential attack including the exact location and quantities of radioactive material, certain detailed design drawings, information on nearby facilities, emergency planning information, and certain assessments of vulnerability and safety analyses.

Required Warning Statement:  Security-Related Information - Withhold Under 10 CFR 2.390

Required Dissemination Control:
