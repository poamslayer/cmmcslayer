Pesticide Producer Survey  
 

  
Category Abbreviation:  PEST

Category Description:  Related to the data gathered regarding the production of pesticides that specifies the non-disclosure of the identity and location of individual producers.

Required Warning Statement:

Required Dissemination Control: 

### Authorities

[7 USC 136i-1(b)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2017-title7/pdf/USCODE-2017-title7-chap6-subchapII-sec136i-1.pdf)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Applicable DoD Policies

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Examples

- Data, including the location from which the data was derived, that would directly or indirectly reveal the identity of individual producers
- Records that contain the product name, amount, approximate date of application, and location of the application of each such pesticide used for a 2-year period after such use
