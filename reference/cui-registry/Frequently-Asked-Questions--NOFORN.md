***Not Releasable to Foreign Nationals (NOFORN)***

***When can I apply NOFORN to a CUI document?***  
 NOFORN can be applied to:  
- unclassified intelligence information properly characterized as CUI  
- Naval Nuclear Propulsion Information (NNPI)  
- DoD Unclassified Controlled Nuclear Information (DCNI)  
- National Disclosure Policy (NDP-1)  
  
Before applying the NOFORN marking, consider whether it would be more appropriate to use a "Releasable to" (REL TO) marking to enhance information sharing.  
  
***Can Permanent Residents or Green Card Holders have access to NOFORN?***  
While Permanent Residents and Green Card Holders are identified as U.S. Persons, they cannot have access to NOFORN information.  
  
***Can dual citizens have access to NOFORN?***  
Provided one of the citizenships is U.S. and the individual is working for or on behalf of the U.S. Government, they can have access to NOFORN.
