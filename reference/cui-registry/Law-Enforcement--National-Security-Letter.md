National Security Letter

  
Category Abbreviation:  LNSL

Category Description:  Related to administrative orders sent to compel the recipients of the letters to provide information to federal investigators. A national security letter (NSL) is a law enforcement investigative tool similar to a subpoena and is most commonly issued by the FBI. NSLs are used to obtain information from companies as part of national security-related investigations.
