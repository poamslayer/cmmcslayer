Internal Data

  
Category Abbreviation:  ID

Category Description:  Refers to information not intended to be disseminated beyond CIA channels involving intelligence activities, sources, or methods. This information may also relate to the CIA's organization, functions, names, official titles, salaries, or numbers of personnel.
