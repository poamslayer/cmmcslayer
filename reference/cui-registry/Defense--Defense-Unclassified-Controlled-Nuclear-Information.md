Unclassified Controlled Nuclear Information - Defense

  
Category Abbreviation:   DCNI  
 

Category Description:  Information relating to Department of Defense special nuclear material (SNM), equipment, and facilities, as defined by *[32 CFR 223](http://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2018-title32-vol2/pdf/CFR-2018-title32-vol2-part223.pdf)*.

Required Warning Statement:

Department of Defense  
Unclassified Controlled Nuclear Information  
Exempt from mandatory disclosure pursuant to 5 U.S.C. 552(b)(3), as authorized by  
10 U.S.C. 128

Required Dissemination Control:
