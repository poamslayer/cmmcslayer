***Training Certificates***

***How do I get my certificate after I complete the CUI mandatory Training?***  
  
If you take the training directly from the [*CDSE*](https://www.cdse.edu/Training/eLearning/IF141/) website, there is an option to create and print a certificate upon completion of the test.  If you take the training through a learning management system, a certificate should be available for you to download and print.  In either case, if you have problems getting a certificate, please talk to your training manager.    
  
There is no certificate available when you take the training through the Security Awareness Hub.
