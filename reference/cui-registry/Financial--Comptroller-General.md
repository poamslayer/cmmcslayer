Comptroller

  
Category Abbreviation:  COMPT

Category Description:  Concerning the Officer of the United States Government charged with duties relating to fiscal affairs, including auditing, examining accounts, and reporting the financial status of the United States Government.

### Examples

- Purchase requests
- MOA/MOU agreements
- Obligations/expenditures
- Operating budget
- Financial business and related documentation
- Inspection results, findings, and deficiencies
- Locations/missions of SOF personnel and elements
- Contracts
- Mission specific training, to include exercise/deployment schedules
- Systems containing financial information
- Specific types of payroll data related to select missions
