***CUI Cover Sheets***  
 

***Is a CUI cover sheet mandatory?***  
  
CUI materials hand-carried out of the office or approved telework location will be placed in an opaque envelope with a CUI cover sheet (*[SF 901](/Portals/109/Documents/Desktop%20Aid%20Docs/SF901-18a%20(CUI%20Cover%20Sheet).pdf?ver=PYPVVZr7NFYfeyc2BGlSQA%3d%3d)*) on top of the documents.  In addition, the cover sheet can be used to protect the document from unauthorized access and observation at any time, but it is not a requirement if the documents are at your desk or in your office.   
  
If using a cover sheet, you must use the official form (*[SF 901](/Portals/109/Documents/Desktop%20Aid%20Docs/SF901-18a%20(CUI%20Cover%20Sheet).pdf?ver=PYPVVZr7NFYfeyc2BGlSQA%3d%3d)*).
