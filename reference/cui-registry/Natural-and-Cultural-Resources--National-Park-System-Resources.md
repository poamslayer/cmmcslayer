National Park System Resources

  
Category Abbreviation:  NPSR

Category Description:  Related to information concerning the nature and specific location of a National Park System resource that is endangered, threatened, rare, or commercially valuable, of mineral or paleontological objects within System units, or of objects of cultural patrimony within System units.
