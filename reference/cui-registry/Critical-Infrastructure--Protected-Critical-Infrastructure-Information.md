Protected Critical Infrastructure Information

  
Category Abbreviation:  PCII

Category Description:  PCII relates to threats, vulnerabilities, or operational experience related to the national infrastructure. PCII offers protection to private sector infrastructure information voluntarily shared with government entities for purposes of homeland security.
