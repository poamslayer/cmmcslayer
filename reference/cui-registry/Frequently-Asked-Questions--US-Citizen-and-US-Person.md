***U.S. Citizen and U.S. Person***  
 

  
 

 

**U.S. Citizen** includes:  
individuals born in the U.S.   
naturalized U.S. citizens  
  
  
**U.S. Person** includes:  
U.S. citizens  
green card holders (see FAQ on *[NOFORN](/Frequently-Asked-Questions/NOFORN/)*)  
lawful permanent residents (see FAQ on *[NOFORN](/Frequently-Asked-Questions/NOFORN/)*)
