Presentence Report

  
Category Abbreviation:  PRE

Category Description:  A report generally prepared to assist the court in determining the most appropriate sentence for a defendant. It can include an assessment of the nature and seriousness of the offense and should contain details summarizing the background information of the defendant and the crime.
