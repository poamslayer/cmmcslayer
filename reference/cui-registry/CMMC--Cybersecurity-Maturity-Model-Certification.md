[**Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification**](https://www.acq.osd.mil/cmmc/index.html)  
  
Building on [Defense Federal Acquisition Regulations Supplement (DFARS) 252.204-7112](https://www.acq.osd.mil/dpap/dars/dfars/html/current/252204.htm), the Office of the Under Secretary of Defense for Acquisition and Sustainment (OUSD(A&S)) is working with DoD stakeholders, University Affiliated Research Centers (UARCs), Federally Funded Research and Development Centers (FFRDC), and industry to develop the Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC).  
  
We refer members of the Defense Industrial Base (DIB) and their partners in the Department working to enhance protection of CUI within the supply chain to the [CMMC Home Page](https://dodcio.defense.gov/CMMC/about/).
