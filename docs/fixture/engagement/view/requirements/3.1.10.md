Use session lock with pattern-hiding displays to prevent access and viewing of data after a period of inactivity.

## 3.1.10[a]

the period of inactivity after which the system initiates a session lock is defined.

| Component | Status | Statement | Remarks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Windows 11 office endpoints | Implemented | Windows 11 endpoints lock the screen after 10 minutes of inactivity. |  |
