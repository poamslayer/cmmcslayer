# Access Control Procedure

Source: Northstar Managed IT template.

## Account provisioning

New accounts are requested by the hiring manager and created by Northstar in Microsoft Entra ID. Access to ShopTrak and the SAG-NAS shares is set up by Nadia Haddad based on the new hire's role.

## Password requirements

Minimum 8 characters, one number, changed every 90 days.

## Unsuccessful logon attempts

3 consecutive failed attempts within 15 minutes lock the account for 15 minutes.

## Account removal

Accounts are disabled within 5 business days of termination. There is no documented step for removing local accounts on SAG-NAS or SHOP-PC-01 specifically.
