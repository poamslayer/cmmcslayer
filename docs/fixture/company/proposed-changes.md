# Proposed Changes

## CloudVault Backup

Northstar Managed IT has proposed CloudVault Backup, a commercial SaaS backup tool, to replace the USB drive backup of SAG-NAS. CloudVault Backup is not on the FedRAMP Marketplace.

## 802.1X and Bluetooth-disable proposal (for 3.1.1, device authorization)

Northstar's proposed fix for the 3.1.1 device authorization gap: deploy 802.1X with certificate authentication on a new managed switch across the whole shop, and disable Bluetooth on every device by policy.

Greg Lindqvist's note with the quote: "This is how we do it at our larger clients. The switch is the bulk of the cost. The Bluetooth policy is free and closes an attack path."

Dana has asked whether this is actually required.
